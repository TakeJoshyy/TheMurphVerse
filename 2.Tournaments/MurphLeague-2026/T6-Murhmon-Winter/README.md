# Murphmon

[MurphLeague 2026](https://github.com/TakeJoshyy/TheMurphVerse/tree/main/2.Tournaments/MurphLeague-2026) Tourney #6 is Heartgold Murphmon - Winter Edition. Base rules and information found [here](https://github.com/TakeJoshyy/TheMurphVerse/tree/main/1.GameModes/Murphmon). 

Murphmon is a randomized Pokemon challenge inspired by Super Kaizo IronMON. It aims to be a bit easier and faster while adding more decision making opportunities to the game. Murphmon can be played 10 different ways, all of which remain officially unbeaten in their base rulesets. Many rules and settings are mindfully adapted for tournament play and the game is intended to be challenging but beatable.

"Winter Edition" will feature a pool of 7 Winter Themed 'Tune-ups'. Competitors will be given 3 'Tuning Tokens' per seed that they may spend on Tune-Ups. 

This Season's Tune-Ups are:
|         Tune-Ups          | Details |
|-|-|
|Break out the good china| You may use all plates freely. |
|Cryokinesis|Earn 1 additional point per gym while running an Ice type or snow warning mon. (must opt-in before first gym for pre-pivot and before Chuck's gym for post-pivot).|
|Winter is coming | Fortify your defenses with up to 10 zincs and irons. Must purchase all at once, any assortment of 10 is ok.|
|Benny's Blankie|An accuracy drop allows the use of swapping (may be activated on the spot but ONLY an accuracy drop allows a swap). May be used throughout the seed.|
|Snow Gear|Allows the use of all glasses, lens, scarves, belts and power items. (May purchase/obtain 1). |
|The North Remembers | Proclaim an early pivot "king" or "queen" to allow catching multiple of the same pivot and kill 2 additional wild(ling)s. |
|Snowday!|This seed is CANCELLED. Re-randomize the seed and pick up where you left off. Counts as 1 additional seed and may only be used once per run.|


# Tourney Details

T6 is hosted by [BennyTots](https://www.twitch.tv/bennytots). Dates: November 5th 11:59 PM EST - November 22nd 11:59 PM EST. Sign up deadline is November 13th  with details on tourney sign up available in the MurphVerse Discord.

> You must be in the [MurphVerse discord](https://discord.gg/fNX4geCU) and all seeds must be live for the entire run and have a VOD available. Murphleague tourneys are free to enter and open to all entrants unless previously banned.

Competitors will be given 75 seeds. Your score is determined by your 3 highest scoring runs combined.
